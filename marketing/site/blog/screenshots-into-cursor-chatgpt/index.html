The fastest way to get screenshots into Cursor, Claude and ChatGPT

By Mayank Harsani, maker of Hopshot · 17 November 2026

Capture straight to the clipboard and paste: Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 on Mac, Win+Shift+S on Windows. When paste fails or you need several images, drag files instead. Here's how to make either route fast without filling your desktop with PNGs.

Why screenshots slow down AI coding

Showing an AI agent the broken UI or the error is faster than describing it. Getting the image into the chat is the slow part:

  • Clipboard paste sometimes silently fails, so you save the file, find it and drag it in.
  • Every save leaves another Screenshot 2026-…png on your desktop.
  • Before-and-after comparisons need two or three images lined up at once.

Free method on Mac

  1. Press Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 and select an area. Adding Ctrl sends it to the clipboard instead of a file.
  2. Click the chat box in Cursor, Claude or ChatGPT and press Cmd+V.
  3. For several images, save them to one folder, select them in Finder and drag them in together.

Free method on Windows

  1. Press Win+Shift+S and select an area.
  2. Paste with Ctrl+V in browser chats and Cursor's chat. In terminal tools such as Claude Code, use Alt+V.
  3. Turn on clipboard history (Win+V) to keep several snips and paste them one by one.

Where the free methods break

The clipboard holds one image at a time, and it is overwritten the moment you copy anything else. Drag-and-drop is reliable but needs a saved file, which brings the clutter back. And in Cursor, dragging the macOS floating thumbnail into the agent chat sometimes attaches nothing.

The faster way: keep screenshots on a shelf

Hopshot sits in your menu bar or system tray. Press ⌘⇧2 on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+2 on Windows, and the capture lands on a floating shelf and is copied to the clipboard at the same time.

  • Paste right away, or drag from the shelf later, even after you've copied other things.
  • Drag into Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Slack, GitHub or any browser upload box.
  • Nothing lands on your desktop. Captures expire after 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days.
  • Click any screenshot on the shelf to copy it again.

Tips for better AI results from screenshots

  • Crop tight. Large captures get downscaled before the model reads them, so small text can get lost.
  • Mark the element you mean with a box or arrow, then add one sentence about what should change.
  • Send before and after shots together when you want a visual change.
