How to send a screenshot from your PC or Mac to your phone (no cable, no email)

By Mayank Harsani, maker of Hopshot · 17 November 2026

On a Mac with an iPhone, use AirDrop. From Windows, use Phone Link or a same-Wi-Fi tool like LocalSend. If you do this every day, or switch between Mac and Windows, a QR code or a paired phone removes the transfer step entirely.

Why this is harder than it should be

  • AirDrop only works between Apple devices.
  • Emailing yourself works everywhere, but takes six steps and fills your inbox.
  • Every free tool needs a saved file, so screenshots pile up on the computer anyway.

Free option 1: AirDrop (Mac to iPhone)

Save the screenshot, right-click it, choose Share → AirDrop and pick your iPhone. It's fast, but Apple-only.

Free option 2: Phone Link (Windows to phone)

Windows' Phone Link app pairs with Android and iPhone. Right-click the screenshot, choose Share, then pick Phone Link. It's built in, but setup takes a while and it connects one PC to one phone.

Free option 3: LocalSend or PairDrop (any device, same Wi-Fi)

Open LocalSend on both devices, or pairdrop.net in both browsers, and send the file. It's great for occasional transfers. You still have to save the screenshot first and pick it each time.

The one-scan way: Hopshot

Hopshot works the same on Windows and Mac. Capture with Ctrl+Shift+2 or ⌘⇧2, then send it to your phone without saving a file.

  • Send to phone: click the phone button on a screenshot and scan the QR code. It opens in your phone's browser with Save and Share buttons. No app needed.
  • Pair once: pair your phone and new captures show up there automatically. Add it to your home screen like an app.
  • Phone to computer too: send a photo from your phone and it lands on your computer's shelf.
  • Private: images are encrypted on your computer before they leave it, and the cloud copy deletes itself (24 hours by default, 7 days at most).

FAQ

Do I need to install an app on my phone?

No. Screenshots open in your phone's browser. You can add Hopshot to your home screen if you want it to feel like an app.

Does it work with Android and iPhone?

Yes. It works in iOS Safari and Android Chrome.

Can Hopshot see my screenshots?

No. Each image is encrypted on your computer, and the key travels only in the link itself, which browsers never send to the server.
