How to screenshot on Windows 11 and 10: every shortcut

By Mayank Harsani, maker of Hopshot · Updated 17 November 2026

The fastest way to screenshot on Windows is Win Shift S: drag over the area you want, then paste it anywhere with Ctrl V. To capture the whole screen and save it as a file, press Win PrtSc. Both work on Windows 11 and Windows 10.

Below is every screenshot shortcut, step-by-step guides for each kind of capture, where Windows saves your screenshots, and fixes for when Print Screen stops working.

How to screenshot part of the screen

  1. Press Win Shift S. The screen dims and a small toolbar appears at the top.
  2. Pick a mode: rectangle, free-form, window or full screen. Rectangle is the default.
  3. Drag over the area. The screenshot is copied to your clipboard.
  4. Press Ctrl V in an email, chat, document or AI tool to paste it.

A notification also pops up. Click it to open the screenshot in Snipping Tool, where you can draw on it, crop it or save it.

How to screenshot the whole screen

  • Save it as a file: press Win PrtSc. The screen dims briefly and the PNG lands in Pictures → Screenshots.
  • Copy it to paste somewhere: on Windows 10, press PrtSc. On Windows 11, press PrtSc and choose full-screen mode in the toolbar, or turn the old behaviour back on (see below).
  • Several monitors: Win PrtSc captures all of them in one wide image. To capture one monitor, use Win Shift S in full-screen mode and click that monitor.

How to screenshot one window

  1. Click the window so it's in front.
  2. Press Alt PrtSc to copy it to the clipboard, or Win Alt PrtSc to save it to Videos → Captures.

Or press Win Shift S, choose window mode and click the window you want.

How to take a screenshot with a delay

Use this to capture menus or tooltips that close when you press a key.

  1. Open the Start menu, type Snipping Tool and open it.
  2. Pick a delay (3, 5 or 10 seconds) from the timer menu, then click New.
  3. Open the menu you want while the timer counts down.

How to screenshot a whole web page

Snipping Tool can't capture a page that scrolls. In Microsoft Edge, press Ctrl Shift S, choose Capture full page, then copy or save the result. In Chrome, use a full-page screenshot extension.

How to screenshot on a laptop

Most laptops put Print Screen on a key shared with another function. If PrtSc does nothing, hold Fn while you press it. You can also skip the key entirely: Win Shift S works on every Windows 10 and 11 laptop.

Windows 11 vs Windows 10

  • Print Screen: on Windows 11 it opens the snipping overlay by default (Microsoft changed this in 2023). On Windows 10 it copies the whole screen to the clipboard.
  • The app: Windows 11 has one Snipping Tool app, which replaced Snip & Sketch. Windows 10 has both Snipping Tool and Snip & Sketch.
  • Auto-save: on Windows 11, snips taken with Win Shift S are also saved to Pictures → Screenshots. On Windows 10 they stay on the clipboard unless you save them.

Screenshot commands and settings

Open the snipping overlay with a command

Press Win R, type ms-screenclip: and press Enter to open the same overlay as Win Shift S. Type snippingtool instead to open the Snipping Tool app. Both work in a desktop shortcut or a script.

Make Print Screen take a plain screenshot again (Windows 11)

  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Keyboard. On some builds it's Bluetooth & devices → Keyboard.
  2. Turn off Use the Print screen key to open screen capture.

Print Screen now copies the full screen to the clipboard, as it did on Windows 10.

Keep several screenshots on the clipboard

Press Win V and turn on clipboard history. Every screenshot you copy is kept, and Win V lets you pick which one to paste.

Copy text out of a screenshot

Open the screenshot in Snipping Tool and click Text actions. You can select and copy the text, or copy all of it at once.

Print Screen not working?

  • Laptop keyboards: press Fn PrtSc, or check whether an F Lock key is on.
  • Another app took the key: OneDrive, Dropbox, Lightshot and Greenshot can grab Print Screen. Check their settings or quit them.
  • Windows 11 opens Snipping Tool instead: that's the default now; see the setting above.
  • Win+Shift+S does nothing: check that Snipping Tool notifications are on in Settings → System → Notifications, then restart.
  • The screenshot is black: some video apps block screenshots of protected content. That's by design.

Where built-in Windows screenshots fall short

  • Pictures → Screenshots fills up. Windows 11 saves every snip, and nothing ever deletes them.
  • Dragging needs a file. To drag a snip into a browser upload box, you have to find it in File Explorer first.
  • The clipboard is overwritten. Copy anything else and your snip is gone, unless you turned on clipboard history.
  • Getting it to your phone is slow. It means Phone Link, a cable or emailing yourself.
  • Different on a Mac. If you also use a Mac, every shortcut changes; see our Snipping Tool for Mac guide.

FAQ

How do I take a screenshot on Windows?

Press Win Shift S, drag over the area you want, then paste it with Ctrl V. To capture the whole screen and save it as a file, press Win PrtSc.

What is the screenshot shortcut on Windows?

Win Shift S snips part of the screen. Win PrtSc saves the whole screen. Alt PrtSc copies just the active window.

Where are screenshots saved on Windows?

In Pictures → Screenshots (C:\Users\your-name\Pictures\Screenshots). Game Bar captures go to Videos → Captures. Shortcuts that only copy to the clipboard don't save a file.

How do I screenshot on a laptop without a Print Screen key?

Use Win Shift S, which needs no Print Screen key. On many laptops Print Screen shares a key with another function, so press Fn PrtSc.

How do I screenshot only one window?

Click the window, then press Alt PrtSc to copy it, or Win Alt PrtSc to save it as a file in Videos → Captures.

Why does Print Screen open the Snipping Tool on Windows 11?

Windows 11 changed the default in 2023. Turn it off in Settings → Accessibility → Keyboard → "Use the Print screen key to open screen capture" (on some builds it's under Bluetooth & devices → Keyboard).

Why is Print Screen not working?

Try Fn+PrtSc, check that another app (OneDrive, Dropbox, Lightshot) hasn't taken the key, and restart. Win+Shift+S works even when Print Screen doesn't.

How do I screenshot a whole web page on Windows?

In Microsoft Edge, press Ctrl+Shift+S and choose Capture full page. Snipping Tool can't capture a scrolling page.

Is there a command to take a screenshot on Windows?

Press Win+R, type ms-screenclip: and press Enter to open the snipping overlay, or type snippingtool to open the Snipping Tool app.

Can I use the same screenshot app on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Hopshot and Snagit both run on Windows and Mac. Hopshot is $10 once and covers up to three computers in any mix.
