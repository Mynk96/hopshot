It leaves encrypted, or it doesn't leave.

Captures stay on your computer until you send one to your phone, share a link, or turn on Sync. When one does leave, it's encrypted here first, and the key never reaches us.

Keys
The decryption key rides in the link's fragment, which browsers never send to a server. We can't read your captures, and neither can anyone who gets into our database.
Expiry
Pick one hour, 24 hours or seven days. Seven is the maximum, and the timer runs whether or not you remember it.

Questions.

When do I get Hopshot?

Founders get beta access in mid-November and the full release in late November 2026. On launch day you get an email with a button that unlocks Hopshot. There's no licence key to copy.

Is this a subscription?

No. Lifetime is a one-time payment. Sync is an optional add-on for phone and multi-computer sync ($24/year after the first year). If you ever cancel Sync, the app keeps working.

Does it work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Windows 10/11 and macOS 13 or newer. Lifetime covers one computer. Lifetime + Sync covers up to three, in any mix of Windows and Mac.

Can you see my screenshots?

No. Nothing leaves your computer unless you send it to your phone, share a link or turn on Sync. When it does, it's encrypted on your device first, and the key never reaches our servers. Cloud copies are deleted automatically (24 hours by default, 7 days at most).

Do I need to install an app on my phone?

No. Your phone opens captures in its browser. You can add Hopshot to your home screen for one-tap access.

How is this different from the built-in screenshot tool?

Built-in tools capture well, then leave you with a file to deal with. Hopshot handles what comes next: dragging into any app, getting it onto your phone, and cleaning up afterwards.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Email us any time before launch for a full refund, no questions asked. If Hopshot hasn't shipped by 31 December 2026, every pre-order is refunded automatically. After launch, refunds are available for 14 days. See the pre-order terms.
