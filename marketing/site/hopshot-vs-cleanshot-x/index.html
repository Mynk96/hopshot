What it costs over three years

CleanShot Basic is a real one-time purchase: pay $35 and the app keeps working. Updates are the part that renews. If you want every update for three years on one Mac, that's $35 + $19 + $19 = $73. Stop renewing and you keep the last version you had. Hopshot is $10 for up to three computers, with every 1.x update.

Stay with CleanShot X if

  • You mark up most screenshots: arrows, blur, text, highlights.
  • You record your screen or make GIFs and tutorials.
  • You need scrolling capture or text recognition (OCR).
  • You only use Macs, and the yearly update renewal doesn't bother you.

Pick Hopshot if

  • You use Windows, or a Mac and a PC, and want the same app on both.
  • Most of your screenshots go straight into Slack, Gmail, ChatGPT, a bug tracker or an upload box.
  • You often need a screenshot on your phone, or a phone screenshot on your computer.
  • You want captures to clean themselves up instead of piling up on your Desktop.
  • You'd rather try it free first, then pay $10 once.

Can you use both?

Yes. You can keep CleanShot X for markup and recording on your Mac, and use Hopshot on a Windows PC or for the captures you only need for a minute. Hopshot's hotkey is ⌘ Shift 2 on Mac and Ctrl Shift 2 on Windows, and you can change it in Settings. FAQ

Is CleanShot X a one-time purchase?

The app is. CleanShot Basic is $35 once and includes one year of updates. After that you keep the version you have, or renew updates for $19 a year. CleanShot Pro is a subscription at $10 per user per month, billed annually.

Does CleanShot X have a free trial?

Not directly: CleanShot offers a 30-day money-back guarantee instead. CleanShot is also part of Setapp, which has a 7-day free trial. Hopshot has a free plan with 10 captures a day, and a 14-day refund on the $10 unlock.

Does Hopshot work on Windows?

Yes. Hopshot runs on Windows 10 and 11 and on macOS 13 or newer, and one $10 purchase covers up to three computers in any mix. CleanShot X is Mac only, and its pricing page says there are no plans for a Windows version.

Can Hopshot annotate, record the screen or take scrolling screenshots?

No. Hopshot captures an area or the full screen and gets it into an app or onto your phone. If you mark up screenshots, record videos or need scrolling capture, CleanShot X is the better tool.

Are Hopshot updates free?

Yes, all 1.x updates are included in the $10 purchase. There is no yearly renewal.
