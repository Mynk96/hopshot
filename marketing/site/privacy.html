Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What we collect on this website

  • Your email address, if you join the launch list, along with the optional answers you give (whether you're interested in Sync and which computer you use) and which page or post you came from.
  • Payment details, if you pre-order. These are collected and processed by our payment provider; we receive your name, email, country and the payment status.
  • Anonymous visit counts through privacy-friendly analytics that use no cookies and collect no personal data.

How we use it

To send your receipt, beta invitation, the email that unlocks Hopshot, and a launch email. We don't sell, rent or share your information, and we don't send marketing beyond what you signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time.

The Hopshot app

Screenshots stay on your computer unless you send one to your phone, share a link or turn on Sync. When a capture leaves your computer, it's encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. The decryption key is never sent to our servers. Encrypted copies are deleted automatically, after 24 hours by default and 7 days at most.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
