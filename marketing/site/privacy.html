Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What we collect on this website

  • Your email address, if you joined our launch list before launch, along with the optional answers you gave (whether you were interested in Sync and which computer you use) and which page or post you came from.
  • Payment details, if you buy Hopshot. These are collected and processed by our payment provider; we receive your name, email, country and the payment status.
  • Website analytics through Google Analytics, so we can see which pages and links bring visitors and which download buttons get used. It records pages viewed, the site or post you came from, your approximate country and device type, and clicks on download buttons. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses, and we turn off its advertising features. Outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it sets first-party cookies (_ga); inside those regions it runs without cookies. This applies to hopshot.xyz only, never to the Hopshot app or the pages that open shared screenshots.

How we use it

To send your receipt, the email that unlocks Hopshot, and any updates you signed up for. We don't sell, rent or share your information, and we don't send marketing beyond what you signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time.

The Hopshot app

Screenshots stay on your computer unless you send one to your phone, share a link or turn on Sync. When a capture leaves your computer, it's encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. The decryption key is never sent to our servers. Encrypted copies are deleted automatically, after 24 hours by default and 7 days at most.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
