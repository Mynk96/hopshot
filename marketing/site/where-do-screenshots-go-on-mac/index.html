Where do screenshots go on Mac? Find them, move them, stop the pile-up

By Mayank Harsani, maker of Hopshot · Updated 23 November 2026

On a Mac, screenshots are saved to your Desktop by default, as PNG files named like Screenshot 2026-11-23 at 10.15.32.png. Screen recordings go to the same place as .mov files. If you changed the save location, or used a shortcut with Control in it, they went somewhere else, and the steps below show you where.

Check where your screenshots are saved

  1. Press ⌘ Shift 5 to open the Screenshot toolbar.
  2. Click Options. Under Save to, the ticked item is where screenshots go: Desktop, Documents, Clipboard, Mail, Messages, Preview, or a folder you picked with Other Location…
  3. Press Esc to close the toolbar without taking a screenshot.

Prefer Terminal? This prints the folder if you ever changed it:

defaults read com.apple.screencapture location

If it says the setting "does not exist", nothing was changed and your screenshots go to the Desktop.

Can't find a screenshot you just took?

Check these in order. Most "missing" screenshots are one of the first three.

  • You copied it instead of saving it. Any shortcut with Control (⌘ Control Shift 4, for example) puts the screenshot on the clipboard only. Paste it with ⌘ V; no file is saved. The same applies if Save to is set to Clipboard.
  • You dragged the floating thumbnail. Dragging the thumbnail into an app hands the image to that app, and nothing is saved to the Desktop. If the drop failed, the screenshot is gone. Why the thumbnail loses screenshots, and the fix.
  • The save location is set to an app. With Preview, Mail or Messages ticked, the screenshot opens in that app instead of becoming a file. Close Preview without saving and it's gone.
  • Your Desktop lives in iCloud. If Desktop & Documents Folders is on in your iCloud Drive settings, screenshots still go to the Desktop, but that Desktop is in iCloud Drive and shows up on your other Macs too. With Optimize Mac Storage on, older ones may be stored in iCloud only and download when you open them.
  • It's there, just buried. Open Finder, click Recents and sort by date, or search "Screenshot". To list every screenshot on the Mac, wherever it is, run this in Terminal:
mdfind "kMDItemIsScreenCapture == 1"

Screenshots stopped saving at all?

Some people found screenshots stopped saving after a macOS update, especially when they touched the thumbnail. Fixes that worked for others, easiest first:

  1. Press ⌘ Shift 5 → Options and untick Show Floating Thumbnail. The file is then saved straight away.
  2. Pick the save location again in Options (choose Desktop, then take a test screenshot).
  3. Restart the Mac. A stuck screenshot service clears more often than you'd expect.
  4. Reset the screenshot settings to Apple's defaults (this also resets your save location and thumbnail choice):
defaults delete com.apple.screencapture
killall SystemUIServer

Check that the shortcuts are still on, too: System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Screenshots. More fixes, by symptom: Mac screenshot not working.

Change where screenshots are saved on Mac

With the Screenshot toolbar

  1. Create a folder for them, for example Pictures → Screenshots.
  2. Press ⌘ Shift 5 → Options → Other Location… and choose the folder.

Screenshots taken with ⌘ Shift 3 and ⌘ Shift 4 follow the same setting.

With Terminal

mkdir -p ~/Pictures/Screenshots
defaults write com.apple.screencapture location ~/Pictures/Screenshots
killall SystemUIServer

To go back to the Desktop, choose Desktop under Options, or run defaults delete com.apple.screencapture location followed by killall SystemUIServer.

Tip: a folder outside Desktop and Documents isn't synced by iCloud's Desktop & Documents feature, so throwaway screenshots don't fill your iCloud storage.

FAQ

Where are screenshots saved on a Mac?

On the Desktop by default, as PNG files named "Screenshot" plus the date and time. To check or change it, press ⌘ Shift 5 and click Options; the ticked item under Save to is the current location.

Why can't I find my screenshot on my Mac?

Usually because it was copied to the clipboard (a shortcut with Control), the floating thumbnail was dragged into an app, or the save location is set to Preview, Mail or Messages. Finder's Recents view, sorted by date, shows the newest screenshots wherever they were saved.

Do Mac screenshots go to the Photos app?

No. Screenshots taken on a Mac are saved as files (on the Desktop by default), not added to Photos. Screenshots from an iPhone or iPad can show up in Photos on your Mac if iCloud Photos is on.

Where do screen recordings go on Mac?

To the same place as screenshots: the Desktop by default, or whatever is set under Save to in the ⌘ Shift 5 Options menu. They are .mov files named "Screen Recording" plus the date and time.

How do I stop screenshots saving to my Mac desktop?

Press ⌘ Shift 5, click Options and choose another folder with Other Location…, or choose Clipboard to save nothing at all. A shelf app such as Hopshot keeps screenshots off the Desktop and deletes them after an hour, a day or a week.
