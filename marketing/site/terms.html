Pre-order terms

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What you're buying

Founder pre-orders are one-time payments in US dollars. There are two options:

  • Lifetime (US$12): a Lifetime license for Hopshot on Windows and macOS, usable on one computer at a time, including all 1.x updates.
  • Lifetime + Sync (US$19): everything in Lifetime, plus twelve months of the Sync add-on starting on the public launch date (Sync allows use on up to three computers while it is active), and access to the beta before the public release. After the first year, Sync is optional and costs US$24 per year.

Founder pricing is limited to the first 200 orders.

Delivery

Hopshot is planned to be released in late November 2026. On launch day, an email with a link that unlocks Hopshot is sent to the address used at checkout. Beta invitations are sent to the same address before launch.

Refunds

You can request a full refund at any time before launch by emailing us from the address you used to pay. No reason is needed. If Hopshot has not been publicly released by 31 December 2026, every founder pre-order is refunded in full automatically. After launch, you can request a refund within 14 days of receiving your license.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card details. Prices are in US dollars; your bank may apply currency conversion.

Changes

If the release date or anything material about the founder offer changes before launch, we'll email every founder, and you can choose a full refund.

Contact

Questions about your order: email us.
