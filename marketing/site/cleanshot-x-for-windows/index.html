CleanShot X for Windows: is there one, and what to use instead

By Mayank Harsani, maker of Hopshot · Updated 21 November 2026

There is no CleanShot X for Windows. It is a Mac-only app, and its own FAQ says there are "no plans" for a Windows version. On Windows, the closest free match is ShareX, and the built-in Snipping Tool (Win Shift S) already covers the basics. If you use a Mac and a PC and want the same app on both, look at Snagit or Hopshot.

Below: which Windows tool replaces each CleanShot feature, the main alternatives compared, and how to pick one if you switch between Mac and Windows.

The best CleanShot X alternatives for Windows

1. ShareX: the closest match, free

ShareX is free and open source, and it does almost everything CleanShot X does: capture, annotate, scrolling capture, video and GIF recording, OCR, pin to screen and upload to dozens of image hosts. The trade-off is the interface. It has hundreds of settings and none of CleanShot's polish. Windows only.

2. Snipping Tool: already on your PC

On Windows 11, Snipping Tool captures areas, windows and the full screen, saves every capture automatically, lets you draw shapes and arrows on it, copies text out of it and records the screen. It has no blur tool and no scrolling capture.

3. Greenshot: quick annotation, free

Greenshot is a light, free, open-source tool for capturing and marking up: arrows, boxes, text, highlight and obfuscate. No screen recording. The Windows version is free; a paid Mac version is in the App Store.

4. Snagit: the paid all-rounder, Mac and Windows

Snagit is the closest commercial match on Windows, with strong editing, scrolling capture, video recording and templates for documentation. It runs on both Windows and Mac, and costs $39 per user per year.

5. Hopshot: for people who capture to share, on Mac and Windows

Hopshot (my app) does a narrower job than CleanShot X. You press a hotkey, the capture lands on a floating shelf, and you drag it into Slack, Gmail, ChatGPT or any upload box, or send it to your phone. Captures clean themselves up after an hour, a day or a week (your choice), so your Desktop stays empty. It has a free plan; $10 once unlocks unlimited use on up to three computers, Mac or Windows. It has no annotation, recording or scrolling capture.

If you use a Mac and a Windows PC

This is the usual reason people search for CleanShot on Windows: they love it on their Mac and now have a work PC too. A CleanShot license only covers Macs, so you'll need a second tool either way. Your options:

  • Keep CleanShot X on the Mac, ShareX on the PC. Free on the Windows side, but two different apps with different shortcuts.
  • Snagit on both. One app and one set of habits, as a yearly subscription.
  • Hopshot on both. One app, the same hotkey idea on each (⌘ Shift 2 on Mac, Ctrl Shift 2 on Windows), and one $10 license for up to three computers. Best if you mostly capture and paste or drag, rather than mark up.

FAQ

Is there a CleanShot X for Windows?

No. CleanShot X only runs on macOS 13 or newer. Its FAQ says there is no Windows version and no plans to release one.

What is the best free CleanShot X alternative for Windows?

ShareX. It is free and open source, and covers most of what CleanShot X does: area capture, annotation, scrolling capture, screen recording, GIFs, text recognition and upload links. It has a lot of settings, so expect a learning curve.

Can I do CleanShot-style screenshots on Windows without installing anything?

Mostly, yes. On Windows 11, Win Shift S opens Snipping Tool's overlay, copies the capture and saves a file to Pictures › Screenshots. Snipping Tool can also draw on screenshots, copy text out of them and record the screen.

Is there a CleanShot alternative that works on both Mac and Windows?

Yes. Snagit runs on both and costs $39 a year per user. Hopshot runs on both, has a free plan, and $10 once unlocks it on up to three computers in any mix of Mac and Windows. ShareX and Shottr are single-platform.

Is CleanShot X a one-time purchase?

The app is: CleanShot Basic is $35 once and includes one year of updates, with optional renewal at $19 a year. CleanShot Cloud Pro is a subscription at $10 per user per month, billed annually. It is also included in a Setapp subscription.

Can I run CleanShot X on Windows in a virtual machine or emulator?

Not in any practical way. CleanShot X is a native Mac app, and capturing your Windows screen needs an app running on Windows itself. Use a Windows tool instead.

Does Windows have something like CleanShot's Quick Access Overlay?

Partly. After Win Shift S, Windows shows a notification you can click to open the capture in Snipping Tool. It disappears after a few seconds and you can't drag it into another app. Hopshot keeps each capture on a floating shelf you can drag from until you're done with it.
