# Design: Pay-first, $10 once, everything included

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-11-12 · Repo: Mynk96/hopshot · Status: APPROVED (founder) · Mode: Startup
Supersedes the pricing in docs/designs/free-shelf-phone-trial.md (free shelf, $19 phone).

## Decision
Free download; the app is locked until **$10 is paid once**. Then everything works forever: capture, shelf, drag, phone send, pairing, Sync (current bundle limits: 3 computers, 3 phones). No trial, no tiers, no subscription. The 14-day no-questions refund stays.

## Premises (all agreed 2026-11-12)
1. Pay-first $10, everything included, as above.
2. **Do not Go live with 0.1.8** (it ships the free shelf). Fold 0.1.8's usage counts, mailing list and feedback into **0.1.9** with the $10 model, so nobody gets a free shelf and then loses it.
3. Onboarding gets a **Pay** step before "Try it".
4. Unpaid 0.1.6/0.1.7 installs lock when they update to 0.1.9; the founder gives comp codes to anyone who matters.
5. marketing/ (hopshot.xyz) is updated too (explicitly in scope).

## Approach chosen: B, one source of truth for the price
- Add `pricing.display` ("$10") to `config/limits.json` (+ shared/Rust types). App UI (locked banner, phone sheet, onboarding, Settings → License, OverLimit, tray "Buy Hopshot — $10"), website (/download, /pricing), release notes (release.yml) and docs read it; marketing/site is hand-edited once.
- The charged price is the Stripe **price id** in `services/api/wrangler.toml` `STRIPE_PRICE_BUNDLE` (checkout uses the id, not a lookup key): create a $10 Price in Stripe, put its id there, deploy the API.
